RCU Intramural Sports — Rules & Policies

Brought to you by RCU Campus Recreation and Rochester Christian Church.
Draft — not yet final. This is a working draft to get the program started. It'll be reviewed and refined before the season begins — if something here doesn't match how RCU actually runs things, that's expected at this stage.

Eligibility

IM Sports are for currently enrolled RCU students (a student must be registered for classes during the semester of the sport they are playing). Student participants must present their student ID at all IM Sports activities. No ID, no play, no exception.

In addition:

  • A player is prohibited from playing with more than one IM Sports team in the same league. Individuals may play with a men's team and a co-rec team, or a women's team and a co-rec team. The IM Sports Office must approve the transfer of players to another roster, and each case is heard individually.
  • Any team that allows an ineligible player to play will forfeit each game in which that player participated.
  • Any team with a player using an assumed name will forfeit that game. The player(s) involved will be ineligible to compete for the remainder of the season or tournament.
  • Each team must submit a finalized roster before the end of the regular season. No changes can be made to rosters after this time.

Intercollegiate & professional athletes

  • Any person who has practiced, competed, or is otherwise recognized as a member of an intercollegiate team is prohibited from participating in IM Sports in that sport or a related sport during that academic year.
  • Former collegiate letter winners are eligible if at least one entire year has elapsed since their last participation with an intercollegiate team. No more than one collegiate letter winner may represent an IM Sports team in their lettered sport.
  • Professional athletes are not allowed to participate in IM Sports in their respective or related sport, unless they have been inactive for five or more calendar years.

Assumption of risk

Individuals are encouraged to have a physical examination and obtain adequate health and accident insurance prior to participating in recreational sports activities. RCU Campus Recreation does not provide insurance coverage for participants.

Participation in IM Sports is voluntary and at each individual's own risk. RCU is not responsible for any injury that may occur to participants in any recreational sports activity.

How to get involved

Residence halls and independent organizations are encouraged to select a manager/team captain to handle operations for their team. An IM Sports information meeting is held at the beginning of each semester — managers/captains get information on upcoming sports, special events, and any policy changes, and are responsible for relaying it to their team.

All official entries are accepted at the IM Sports Office until the posted deadline, on the proper entry form, with contact information filled in completely. Refundable forfeit fees are due at entry. Schedules are prepared and distributed at the captains' meeting for each sport.

Classification of divisions

Activities are divided into three divisions for which all champions are determined. Not all divisions exist for every activity.
Women's Division

Individuals/teams of eligible female undergraduate/graduate students. Participants may also play simultaneously in the co-rec division.

Men's Division

Individuals/teams of eligible male undergraduate/graduate students. Participants may also play simultaneously in the co-rec division.

Co-Rec Division

Individuals/teams of eligible female and male undergraduate/graduate students. Participants may also play simultaneously in a women's or men's division activity.

Sportsmanship

All participants are obligated to conduct themselves in a manner that fosters a safe, friendly, and enjoyable atmosphere for players and spectators. RCU Campus Recreation will not tolerate actions considered prejudiced, rude, disrespectful, or unsportsmanlike.

Misconduct

Any individual involved in fighting, verbal or physical abuse, or purposefully harming another participant, spectator, or IM employee is subject to being banned from IM Sports and possibly reported to the Dean of Students Office.

Alcohol & controlled substances

Alcohol and controlled substances are not permitted on or around IM Sports playing areas. Anyone appearing to be under the influence will not be allowed to participate, and will be reported to the Dean of Students Office for possible judicial sanctions.

Tobacco products

The use of tobacco products on or around IM Sports fields is prohibited.

The IM Sports staff reserve the right — and are obligated — to disallow participants or spectators showing signs of intoxication, or in possession of alcohol or controlled substances, from any IM Sports activity or event. If a team receives three (3) unsportsmanlike penalties in a game, that results in an automatic forfeiture and an unacceptable rating; the team captain must meet with the Sports and Recreation Program Coordinator before the team may continue in that sport.

Team sportsmanship rating system

An objective 4-to-0 scale assessing team attitude and behavior before, during, and after every contest, based on sportsmanship infractions (SI) — unsportsmanlike penalties, technical fouls, or yellow/red cards. 1 SI = one penalty, technical foul, or yellow card; 2 SI = one red card, two yellow cards, two penalties, or two technical fouls. A team keeping at least a 3 average throughout the league is eligible for playoffs, and must stay at 3 or higher to keep advancing.

Game ending sportsmanship

  • Team members did not meet eligibility requirements
  • Playing with participants on the outstanding ejection list, or suspended from IM Sports
  • Public indecency, vulgarity, or obscenity
  • Playing after consuming alcohol/drugs — if discovered mid-contest, the player(s) are removed immediately and the contest may be forfeited to the opponent

  • Physical abuse — fighting or wrestling with an opponent/teammate, before, during, or after a contest
  • Any threatening behavior (verbal or nonverbal) toward any RCU IM Sports staff, participant, or spectator

  • Any violation of the RCU Student Code of Conduct

  • Failing to cooperate with IM Sports staff/University officials, or misrepresenting/withholding requested information

IM Sports officials/supervisors determine ratings; appeals of unacceptable ratings are not recognized. The Sports and Recreation Program Coordinator determines season-ending ratings and may review any rating given.

Enforcement

Teams and individuals may be suspended — at the discretion of the Sports and Recreation Program Coordinator — from one game up to the remainder of their time at RCU. A suspended player remains so until a meeting and formal reinstatement by the coordinator.

A player ejected from a contest must meet with the coordinator before continuing in IM Sports (appointments must be scheduled), and must serve a minimum one-game suspension in addition to any other sanctions.

A suspension takes effect immediately. It may be appealed in writing to the associate director for IM within 48 hours; the associate director forwards the appeal to the Intramural Sports Advisory Board chairperson, whose decision is final. The coordinator will decide all disciplinary cases within 48 hours of the incident.

Forfeits & defaults

Teams are expected to be ready to play at game time. A team failing to report at the scheduled start forfeits to their opponent; if both teams fail to report, each is given a forfeit and the game is not rescheduled. This applies to all team, individual, and special event contests.

  • Types of forfeits: improper uniform, no-show, arriving after the grace period, playing an ineligible player, not calling in before the default time (1 p.m. day of game; Friday 1 p.m. for a Sunday game), or acts of poor sportsmanship.
  • Sportsmanship rating for forfeits: neither the forfeiting nor the winning-by-forfeit team receives a sportsmanship rating for that game (unless the forfeit was called for behavior).
  • Limit of forfeits: two forfeits in a regular season drops a team from future competition.
  • Tournament forfeit: eliminates a team from tournament play entirely.
  • Grace period: only applies if stated in the sport's specific rules; the clock keeps running and points are awarded per those rules. If the grace period expires with no game started, it's forfeited to the opponent (or a double forfeit if neither side is ready).
  • Winning by forfeit: requires the minimum number of players for that sport, present and ready to play at the scheduled time and location.
  • Defaults: only a team captain may request an advance default, in person, by 1 p.m. the day of the contest (Friday 5 p.m. for a weekend game), directed to the coordinator. A default counts as a loss on the record, but the team stays in competition and remains eligible for the refundable forfeit fee at season's end.

Scheduling, postponements & rescheduling

IM Sports Staff prepare all regular season and playoff schedules; direct scheduling concerns to the Sports and Recreation Program Coordinator as soon as possible. Teams are eligible for playoffs if they finish the regular season, had a representative at the captains' meeting, and have not forfeited twice — playoff games may run on different nights/times than the regular season.

Postponements are rare and require agreement from both team captains plus the coordinator's approval; the team requesting the postponement is responsible for coordinating a new time, or must forfeit if one can't be arranged.

Weather postponements are decided by 2:30 p.m. the day of the contest, with captains notified by phone or email. IM Sports will attempt to reschedule; if a regular-season contest can't be rescheduled, both teams are credited a win. Playoff postponements are always rescheduled.

Rescheduling is discouraged given limited time and facility space, with no guarantee a game can be moved. The procedure: call your opponent to request a reschedule, then both captains must meet together with the coordinator at the IM Sports Office (Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m.) to agree a new time — completed by 5 p.m. the day before the game (noon the Friday before, for Sunday games). Games called for weather may instead be rescheduled by meeting at the office to pick a make-up date.

Coordinator handbook

The set-up order that trips up a first season if skipped — in the sequence you'll actually need it.
1

Get administrative buy-in & a budget line

Confirm who intramurals reports to (Campus Rec or Student Life), and get a dollar figure before committing to equipment or officials' pay.

2

Reserve facilities before announcing dates

Gym and field time is shared with varsity practice, PE classes, and chapel events — lock in the weekly block first.

3

Liability waivers & insurance

Every participant signs a waiver before their first game, no exceptions. Confirm with RCU risk management whether campus insurance covers intramural injuries.

4

Pick a scheduling tool

This app covers sign-ups, rosters, and round-robin scheduling for season one — revisit as the program grows.

5

Recruit & pay officials

Student refs are the standard model — budget an hourly rate and run one rules/mechanics training before the season starts.

6

Write a short rulebook per sport

Adapt from NIRSA rule sets and note house rules (mercy rule, co-rec ratios) clearly.

7

Set eligibility & conduct policy

Enrolled students (staff/faculty in a co-rec division), a sportsmanship code, and a clear ejection/forfeit policy communicated up front.

8

Promote sign-ups 2–3 weeks out

Chapel announcements, RA/hall reps, and direct outreach to floor groups tend to outperform email alone.

9

Run the season, then debrief

Short end-of-season survey to captains — what to keep, what to fix before the next 8-week cycle.

Announcement

Shows in a banner at the top of the Home tab for every visitor — a rained-out game, a schedule change, whatever's time-sensitive. Leave it blank to hide the banner.

Now playing

Drives the Home ticker, the sign-up shortcut, and the "Active" badge on the Sports tab for every visitor.

Sports

Add, remove, rename, or duplicate sports freely — run two volleyball leagues, drop a sport for the season, add a new indoor one, whatever the campus calendar needs. This list drives the Sports tab, the Sign Up sport picker, the Schedule tab, and Game Times below.

Season dates

Set when the season actually starts (Week 1) and how many weeks of play it needs — sign-ups stay open the whole time before that, and every week's date on the Home timeline and every matchup's week date on the Schedule tab is calculated from these automatically.

Includes regular season, playoffs, and championship — at least 3. Sign-ups are simply open any time before Week 1, with no separate deadline to set.

A break skips that whole calendar week — no games, and every week after it shifts out by one week automatically. Pick any date inside the week you want off.

Devotional plan

Points the Devotional tab at a Life.Church Bible App reading plan (bible.com/reading-plans/…). One day advances per calendar day from the season's start date, wrapping back to Day 1 after the last day — swap this to a new plan whenever the current one finishes.

Leave day details blank (or shorter than the day count) to just show "Day N" for the missing days — the link to the Bible App works either way.

Game times

Assign a date, time, and location to each matchup. Saved times appear on the Schedule tab for everyone.
